# Vannevar Bush

## Summary

American engineer and science administrator. Best known to the personal-knowledge-management
community for his 1945 essay "As We May Think," which proposed the [[memex]] — a personal
knowledge store with associative trails between documents. Bush's vision was private and
actively curated, with the connections between documents as valuable as the documents
themselves — closer in spirit to the modern [[llm-wiki-pattern]] than to what the web
became.

## Key facts

- Authored "As We May Think" in *The Atlantic*, July 1945
- Proposed the [[memex]] in that essay
- The part of his vision he couldn't solve was the maintenance — who keeps the associative trails current as documents and ideas accumulate
