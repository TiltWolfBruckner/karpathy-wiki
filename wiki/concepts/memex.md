# Memex

## Definition

A hypothetical personal knowledge device proposed by [[vannevar-bush]] in 1945, that would
store a person's books, records, and communications and let them follow associative trails
between documents. Less a specific machine than a model for a personal, curated, deeply
linked knowledge store.

## Why it matters

The Memex is the spiritual ancestor of personal knowledge management, hypertext, and — per
[[llm-wiki|2026-05-11]] — the [[llm-wiki-pattern]]. Bush correctly identified that the
connections between documents matter as much as the documents themselves; what he couldn't
solve was who maintains the connections as the corpus grows. The web took hypertext in a
different direction (global, open, uncurated); the Memex's private-and-curated emphasis is
now feasible again, with LLMs handling the maintenance burden Bush left unsolved.
